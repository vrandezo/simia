My thoughts on Alignment research

Alignment research seeks to ensure that hypothetical future superintelligent AIs will be beneficial to humanity—that they are “aligned” with “our goals,” that they won’t turn into Skynet or universal paperclip factories. But these AI systems will be embedded in larger processes and organizations. And the problem is: we haven’t even figured out how to align those systems with human values.
