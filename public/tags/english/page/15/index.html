It starts entirely black and then listens to Wikidata edits. Every time an item with a coordinate is edited, a blue dot in the corresponding place is made. So slowly, over time, you get a more and more complete map of Wikidata items. https://vrandezo.github.io/wikidata-edit-map/ If you open the developer console, you can get links and names of the items being displayed.
