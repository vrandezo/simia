The Web of Knowledge

I thought I knew the history of Wikipedia inside and out. Then I read Giota Alevizou’s The Web of Knowledge, and I found myself honestly surprised. I learned things about Wikipedia and its role in the knowledge ecosystem that had a major impact on my career, and that I was completely unaware of. 2026 is Wikipedia’s Jubilee year, and there are a few books published retelling the history of Wikipedia. But this book goes further; it discusses the role of Wikipedia in the wider knowledge infrastructure, and it encompasses the history of (English) digital encyclopedias as a whole, covering in particular the journeys of Britannica and Encarta from the 1990s on, in addition to telling the history of Wikipedia....
