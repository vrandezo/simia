OK

I often hear “don’t go for the mediocre, go for the best!”, or “I am the best, * the rest” and similar slogans. But striving for the best, for perfection, for excellence, is tiring in the best of times, never mind, forgive the cliché, in these unprecedented times. Our brains are not wired for the best, we are not optimisers. We are naturally ‘satisficers’, we have evolved for the good-enough. For this insight, Herbert Simon received a Nobel prize, the only Turing Award winner to ever get one....
