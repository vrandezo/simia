World Wide Prolog

Today I had an idea - maybe this whole Semantic Web idea is nothing else than a big worldwide Prolog program. It’s the AI researchers trying to enter the real world through the W3Cs backdoor… No, really, think about it: almost all most people do with OWL is actually some logic programing. Declaring subsumptions, predicates, conjunctions, testing for entailment, getting answers out of this - but on a world wide scale....
