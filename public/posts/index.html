Patrick Stewart

When the table read of the first episode of The Next Generation happened, it was Patrick Stewart who first pronounced the name of the android Data. He enunciated a crisp T instead of the American habit of letting the letter disappear somewhere on the top of their mouth. Gene Roddenberry then set up the rule that whoever reads a name first decides on the pronunciation.
