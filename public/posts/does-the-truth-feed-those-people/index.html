Does the Truth feed those people?

October 7, 2026

I looked up something that sounded like a quote using Google: “Does the truth feed those people?”

Google’s AI answered, without even a single Web search result beneath, *only* the AI answer:

“Does the truth feed those people?” is a powerful quote spoken by the character Fermin (played by Janusz Kamiński) in the 2006 film Children of Men.

Which would be a great answer.

If that was a quote from the film. It isn’t.

If there was a character named Fermin in that film. There isn’t.

If Janusz Kamiński had played in that film. He didn’t.

If Janusz Kamiński was even an actor. He isn’t. He is a cinematographer and director.

If Janusz Kamiński had anything to do with the film. He didn’t.

It has been a while since I noticed such a complete fail. At first I believed the answer, and started to act on it. Only later I noticed that it didn’t sit right. And it unraveled.

Next time it might not unravel so easy.
