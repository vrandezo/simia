Workshop

A workshop is a type of event. In computer science, it usually describes a side-event of a conference, often held right before or after the main conference, about a specific topic of the conference. The submissions are often not as polished as for the conference, but thus often also more interesting and novel. See also :Category:Workshop. Same as Q40444998.

Free data

If you republish data, it’s a bit more complicated, but not as horrible as you might think. Imagine a student reading a CC-BY-SA published textbook on compilers.
