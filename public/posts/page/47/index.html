What's DLP?

There is the almighty OWL Full, there’s OWL DL, the easy* OWL Lite, and then there are numerous ‘proprietary’ expansions, which are more (OWL-E) or less (OWL Flight) compatible and useful. New versions: owlrdf2owlxml, dlpconvert

New versions of owlrdf2owlxml and dlpconvert are out. owlrdf2owlxml got renamed, as it was formerly known as rdf2owlxml. But as a colleague pointed out, this name can easily be misunderstood, meaning to transform arbitrarily RDF to OWL. It doesn’t do that, it only transforms OWL to OWL, from RDF/XML-serialisation to XML Presentation Syntax. And it seems to work quite stable, it can even transform the famous wine ontology. Version 0.4 out now....
