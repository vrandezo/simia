Simia technology

Currently, the blog runs on Hugo with PageMod, and planned to use PageCMS and GitHub.

For almost 20 years this blog was running on Semantic MediaWiki.

Simia was run on MediaWiki with the Semantic MediaWiki and Blog2RSS extension enabled. I plan to describe here more details of the technology and what it allows to do, and how it can be used on your own webspace, but for now I just point to a number of nice pages to demonstrate some of the possibilities:

  • A timeline view of all posts that have been published on the Nodix blog
  • A timeline view of all comments to the blogs here
  • A list of all blog posts – alphabetically
  • A list of a few feeds created on this page – creating new feeds is easy
  • An example of a page with a number of comments

Blog2RSS is an extension to MediaWiki, that requires Semantic MediaWiki. It allows to use Semantic MediaWiki’s query mechanism to create RSS feeds.

Blog2RSS was never published.

In good web tradition you can view the source of any page (and I don’t mean the HTML source, which your browser allows you to view anyway, I mean the wiki source of the page): just go to the bottom of the page and click on the view source link.
