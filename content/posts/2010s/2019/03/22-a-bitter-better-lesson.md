Rich Sutton is expressing some frustration in his short essay on
computation and simple methods beating smart methods again and again.

  - [A bitter
    lesson](http://www.incompleteideas.net/IncIdeas/BitterLesson.html)

Rodney Brooks answers with great arguments on why this is not really the
case, and how we're just hiding human ingenuity and smartness better.

  - [A better lesson](https://rodneybrooks.com/a-better-lesson/)

They're both mostly right, and it was interesting to read the arguments
on both sides. And yet, not really new - it's mostly rehashing the
arguments from [The unreasonable effectiveness of
data](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/4804817) by Alon Halevy,
Peter Norvig, and Fernando Pereira ten years ago. But nicely updated and
much shorter. So worth a read\!
