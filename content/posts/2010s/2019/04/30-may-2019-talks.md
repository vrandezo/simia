I am honored to give the following three invited talks in the next few
weeks:

  - [Knowledge Graph
    Conference](http://sps.columbia.edu/executive-education/knowledge-graph-conference),
    Columbia University, New York, May 7, 2019
  - [Workshop on Knowledge Graph Technology and
    Applications](https://datainnovation.soic.indiana.edu/www2019_kgta/index.html),
    co-located with The Web Conference in San Francisco, May 13, 2019
  - [Wiki Workshop 2019](http://wikiworkshop.org/2019/), co-located with
    The Web Conference in San Francisco, May 14, 2019

The topics will all be on Wikidata, how the Wikipedias use it, and the
Abstract Wikipedia idea.
