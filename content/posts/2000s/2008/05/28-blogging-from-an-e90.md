After pondering it for far too long, I finally got a new mobile phone: a
Nokia E90. It is pretty big and heavy, but I don't mind really. I am
looking at it as a light-weight laptop replacement. But I am not sure I
will learn to love the keyboard, really. Experimenting.

But since it has a full keyboard, programming in Python is indeed an
option. I had Python on my previous phone too, but heck, T9 is not cool
to type code.
