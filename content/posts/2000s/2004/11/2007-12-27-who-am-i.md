Well, as this being a blog, it will turn out that it is more important
what I write than who I am. Just for the context, I nevertheless want to
offer a short sketch about my bio.

I studied Computer Science and Philosophy at the [University of
Stuttgart](University_of_Stuttgart "wikilink"), Germany. In Computer
Science, I thought about Software Architectures, Programming Languages
and User Interfaces, and my master thesis happened to be the first
package to offer a [validating XML parser for the programming language
Ada 95](XML4ADA95 "wikilink").  
In Philosophy I started thinking a lot of Justice, especially John Rawls
and Plato, but finally I had a strong move to Construcitivst
Epistemology and the ontological status of neural networks (both papers
are in German and available from my [website](Nodix "wikilink").

It's a pretty funny thing that next week I will listen to talk on neural
networks and ontologies again, and nevertheless my then made paper and
the talk won't have too much in common ;-)

Well, so how comes I am working on Semantic Web technologies by now? I
have the incredible luck to work in the [Knowledge Management
Group](http://www.aifb.uni-karlsruhe.de/WBS) of the
[AIFB](AIFB "wikilink") in Karlsruhe, and there on the EU
[SEKT](SEKT "wikilink") Project. I still have a lot to learn, but in the
last few weeks I aggregated quite a good grasp on Ontology Engineering,
RDF and OWL and some other fields. This is all pretty exicting and
amazing and I am looking forward to see what's around the next triple.
