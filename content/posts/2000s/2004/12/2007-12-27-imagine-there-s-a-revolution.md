... and no one is going to it.

This notion sometimes scares me when I think abou the semantic web. What
if all this great ideas are just to complex to be implemented? What if
it remains an ivory tower dream? But, on the other hand, how much
pragmatism can we take without loosing the vision?

And then, again, I see the semantic web working already: it's
[del.icio.us](http://del.icio.us), it's [flickr](http://flickr.com),
it's [julie](http://crschmidt.net/julie/), and there's so much more to
come. The big time of the semantic web is yet to come, and I think none
of us can really imagine the impact it is going to have. But it will
definitively be interesting\!
