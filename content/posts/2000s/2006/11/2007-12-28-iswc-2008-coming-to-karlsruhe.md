Yeah\! [ISWC2006](http://iswc2006.semanticweb.org/) is just starting,
and I am really looking forward to it. The
[schedule](http://schedule.semanticweb.org/) looks more than promising,
and [Semantic MediaWiki](Semantic_MediaWiki "wikilink") is among the
finalists for the [Semantic Web
Challenge](http://challenge.semanticweb.org/)\! I will write more about
this year's ISWC the next few days.

But, now the news: yesterday it was decided that
[ISWC2008](ISWC2008 "wikilink") will be hosted by the
[AIFB](AIFB "wikilink") in Karlsruhe\! It's a pleasure and a honor --
and I am certainly looking forward to it. Yeah\!
