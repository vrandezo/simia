I have been the week in Budva, Montenegro, at the
[ESWC2006](http://www.eswc2006.org/). It was lovely. The keynotes were
inspiring, the talks had a good quality, and the [SemWiki
workshop](http://www.semwiki.org/) was plain great. Oh, and the
[Semantic
Wikipedia](http://www.eswc2006.org/poster-papers/FP11-Voelkel.pdf) won
the best poster award\!

But what is much more interesting is the magic that [Tom
Heath](http://wiki.eswc2006.org/index.php/Tom_Heath), the Semantic Web
Technologies Co-ordinator, managed: the ESWC website is a [showcase of
Semantic Web technologies](http://www.eswc2006.org/technologies/)\! A
[wiki](http://wiki.eswc2006.org/), a [photo annotation
tool](http://photos.eswc2006.org/), a [chat](http://chat.eswc2006.org/),
a [search](http://search.eswc2006.org/), a [bibliography
server](http://www.bibsonomy.org/user/stumme/eswc+2006), a [rich
semantic client](http://www.dbin.org/downloads.php), an
[ontology](http://www.eswc2006.org/technologies/ontology), the award
winning \[<http://prauw.cs.vu.nl:8080/flink-eswc/>? flink\]... try it
out\!

Now I am in Croatia, and taking my first real break since I started on
the Semantic Web. Offline for three weeks.

Yay.
