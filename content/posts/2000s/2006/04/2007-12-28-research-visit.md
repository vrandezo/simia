So, I have arrived for my very first longer research visit. I am staying
at the [Laboratory of Applied
Ontologies](http://www.loa-cnr.it/index.html) in Rome. I have never been
to Rome before, and all my type of non-work experience I'll chat about
in [my regular blog](Nodix "wikilink"), in German. Here I'll stick to
Semantic Stuff and such.

So, if you are nearby and would like to meet -- give me a note\! I am
staying in Rome up to the [WWW](http://www2006.org/), i.e. up to May
20th. The plan is to work on my dissertation topic, Ontology Evaluation,
especially in the wake of the [EON2006
workshop](http://km.aifb.uni-karlsruhe.de/ws/eon2006), but that's not
all it seems. People are interested in and knowledge about Semantic
Wikis as well. So there will be quite a lot stuff happening in the next
few weeks -- I'm excited about it all.

Who knows what will happen? If my plan works out, at the end of the stay
we will have a common framework for Ontology Evaluation. And I am not
talking about this paper frameworks -- you know, that are presented in
papers with titles starting *"Towards a..."* or *"A framework for..."*.
No, but real software, stuff you can download, and play with.
