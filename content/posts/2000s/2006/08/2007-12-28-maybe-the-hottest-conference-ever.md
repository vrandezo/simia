The [Wikipedia Hacking
Days](http://wikimania2006.wikimedia.org/wiki/Hacking_Days) are over. We
have been visiting [Siggraph](http://www.siggraph.org/s2006/), we had a
tour through the [MIT Media Lab](http://www.media.mit.edu/), some of the
people around were [Brion
Vibber](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/User:Brion_VIBBER) (Wikimedia's
CTO), [Ward Cunningham](http://c2.com/ward/) (the guy who invented
wikis), [Dan Bricklin](http://www.bricklin.com/) (the guy who invented
spreadsheets), [Aaron Swartz](http://www.aaronsw.com/) (a web
wunderkind, he wrote the RSS specs at 14), [Jimbo
Wales](http://blog.jimmywales.com/) (the guy who made Wikipedia happen),
and many other people. We have been working at the [One Laptop per
Child](http://laptop.org/) offices, the office to easily the coolest
project of the world.

During our stay at the Hacking Days, we had the chance to meet up with
the local IBM Semantic Web dev staff and [Elias
Torres](http://torrez.us/), who showed us the fabulous work they are
doing right now on the Semantic Web technology stack (never before rapid
application deployment was so *rapid*). And we also met up with the
[Simile project people](http://simile.mit.edu/), where we talked about
connecting their stuff like Longwell and Timeline to the [Semantic
MediaWiki](Semantic_MediaWiki "wikilink"). We actually tried Timeline
out on the [ISWC2006 conference
page](http://wiki.ontoworld.org/index.php/ISWC2006), and the RDF worked
out of the box, giving us a timeline of the workshop deadlines. Yay\!

Today started [Wikimania2006](http://wikimania2006.wikimedia.org/) at
the [Harvard Law School](http://www.law.harvard.edu/). was not only a
keynote by [Lawrence Lessig](http://www.lessig.org/), as great as
expected, but also our [panel on the Semantic
Wikipedia](http://wikimania2006.wikimedia.org/wiki/Proceedings:DV2). We
had an unexpected guest (who didn't get introduced, so most people
didn't even realize he was there), [Tim
Berners-Lee](http://www.w3.org/People/Berners-Lee/), probably still
jetlagged from a trip to Malaysia. The session was received well, and
Brion said, that he sees us on the way of getting the extension into
Wikipedia proper. Way cool. And we got bug reports from Sir Timbl again.

And there are still two days to go. If you're around and like to meet,
drop a note.

Trust me — it all sounds like a dream to me.
