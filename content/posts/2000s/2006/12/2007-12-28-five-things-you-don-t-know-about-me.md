Well, I don't think I have been tagged yet, but I could be within the
next few days (the [meme is
spreading](http://blogsearch.google.com/blogsearch?q=5+things+you+dont+know+about+me&scoring=d)),
and as I won't be here for a while, I decided to strike preemptively. If
no one tags me, I assume to take one of
[danah's](http://www.zephoria.org/thoughts/archives/2006/12/28/end_of_the_year.html).

So, here we go:

1.  I was born without fingernails. They grew after a few weeks. But
    nevertheless, whenever they wanted to cut my nails when I was a kid,
    no one could do it alone -- I always panicked and needed to be held
    down.
2.  Last year, I contributed to four hardcover books. Only one of them
    was scientific. The rest were modules for Germany's most popular
    role playing game, [The Dark Eye](Das_Schwarze_Auge "wikilink").
3.  I am a total optimist. OK, you knew that. But you did not know that
    I actually tend to forget everything bad. Even in songs, I noticed
    that I only remember the happy lines, and I forget the bad ones.
4.  I co-author a webcomic with my sister, the
    [nutkidz](nutkidz "wikilink"). We don't manage to meet any schedule,
    but we do have a storyline. I use the characters quite often in my
    presentations, though.
5.  I still have an account with [Ultima Online](http://www.uo.com/)
    (although I play only three or four times a year), and I even have a
    [CompuServe
    Classic](http://www.compuserve.com/gateway/communications/default.asp)
    account -- basically, because I like the chat software. I did not
    get rid of my old PC, because it still runs the old CompuServe
    Information Manager 3.0. I never figured out how to run IRC.
