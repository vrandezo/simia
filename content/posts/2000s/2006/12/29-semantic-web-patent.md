[Tim
Finin](http://ebiquity.umbc.edu/blogger/when-did-the-semantic-web-enter-our-lexicon/)
and [Jim
Hendler](http://www.mindswap.org/blog/2006/12/27/earliest-use-of-the-term-semantic-web/)
are asking about the earliest usage of the term Semantic Web. [Tim
Berners-Lee](http://www.w3.org/People/Berners-Lee/) (who else?) spoke
about the need of [*semantics in the
web*](http://www.w3.org/Talks/WWW94Tim/) at the WWW 1994 plenary talk in
Geneva, though the term Semantic Web does not appear there directly.
Whatever. What rather surprised me, though, is, when surfing a bit for
the term, I discovered that [Amit
Sheth](http://lsdis.cs.uga.edu/~amit/), host of this year's
[ISWC](http://iswc2006.semanticweb.org/), filed the patent on it, back
in 2000: [System and method for creating a Semantic
Web](http://www.google.com/patents?vid=USPAT6311194). My guess would be,
that is the oldest patent of it.
