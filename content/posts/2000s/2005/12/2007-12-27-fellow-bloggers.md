Just a few pointers to people with blogs I usually follow:

  - [Max Völkel](http://xamde.blogspot.com/), a colleague from the
    [AIFB](AIFB "wikilink"), soon moving to the [FZI](FZI "wikilink")
    and right now visiting [DERI](DERI "wikilink"). He obviously likes
    groups with acronyms. And he's a fun read.
  - [Valentin Zacharias](http://vzach.blogspot.com/), who has deeper
    thoughts on this whole Semantic Web stuff than most people I know,
    working at the [FZI](FZI "wikilink"). He's often a thought-provoking
    read.
  - [Planet RDF](http://planetrdf.com/). The \#1 blog on news for the
    (S/s)emantic (W/w)eb, both with major and minor initials. That's
    informative.
  - [Nick Kings](http://inkeyes.blogspot.com/) from [BT
    exact](http://www.btplc.com/Innovation/BTExact.htm). We are working
    together on the [SEKT](SEKT "wikilink") project, and he just started
    to blog. Welcome\! A long first post. But the second leads to a
    great video\!
  - [Brendan Eich](http://weblogs.mozillazine.org/roadmap/), one of the
    [Mozilla](http://www.mozilla.org/) gurus. I want to know where
    Mozilla is headed to - so I read his musings.
  - [PhD](http://www.phdcomics.com/). It's not a person, it's a
    webcomic, granted, but they offer a RSS feed for the comic. Cool. I
    always look forward for new episodes.

So, if you think I should read you, drop me a note. I especially like
peers, meaning, people who like I do are working on the Semantic Web,
maybe PhD students, and who don't know the answer to anything, but like
to work on it, making the web come real.
