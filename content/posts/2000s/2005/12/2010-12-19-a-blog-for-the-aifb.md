Although I blogged here about the [AIFB](AIFB "wikilink") as well - the
great place I am working at - [Jens
Hartmann](http://www.aifb.uni-karlsruhe.de/Personen/viewPerson?id_db=90)
suggested to create an own, dedicated
[AIFB-Blog](http://blog.ontoworld.org/) on
[ontoworld](ontoworld "wikilink"). It's in beta still, kind of. We hope
that other AIFB-people will blog there as well, and so keep you up to
date with AIFB-stuff, blog about our papers, workshops, conference
attendances, break-through results, but also the great weather in
Karlsruhe and stories that happened here.

So, while I will still continue to post on the Semantic Web here, the
more workplace related stuff will be found
[there](http://blog.ontoworld.org/): at the new
[AIFB-Blog](http://blog.ontoworld.org/).
