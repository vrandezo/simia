Wow, I never can't get enough FOAF :) Besides my [Nodix-FOAF
file](http://nodix.de/foaf.rdf) the [AIFB
Portal](http://www.aifb.uni-karlsruhe.de/) now also offers a [FOAF
export](http://www.aifb.uni-karlsruhe.de/Personen/viewPersonFOAF/foaf_2097.rdf)
for all the [people at the
AIFB](http://www.aifb.uni-karlsruhe.de/Personen/english) (using URIs for
the people besides the mailbox SHA1-Sum as identifiers. Hah\! FOAFers
won't like that, but [TimBL told us to do it this
way](http://www.w3.org/2005/Talks/1110-iswc-tbl/#\(4\)) in [Galway a few
weeks ago](http://iswc2005.semanticweb.org/)).

If you point your smushers at the FOAFs, I wonder if you can also
compile the
[SWRC-output](http://www.aifb.uni-karlsruhe.de/Personen/viewPersonOWL/id2097.owl)
into it, as they use the same URI? And can you also, by adding my own
[FOAF from Nodix](http://nodix.de/foaf.rdf), that I am the same person?
Anyone dare's to try? :)

It's a pity [Morten's FOAF explorer is
down](http://xml.mfd-consult.dk/foaf/explorer/), I'd really like to try
it out and browse. Isn't there something similar out there?

A tidbit more on that is also [posted on the AIFB
blog](http://blog.ontoworld.org/?p=8), but from a different point of
view.
