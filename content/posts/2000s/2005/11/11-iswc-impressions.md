The [ISWC 2005](http://iswc2005.semanticweb.org/) is over, but I'm still
in Galway, hanging around at the [OWL Experiences and Direction
Workshop](http://www.mindswap.org/2005/OWLWorkshop/). The ISWC was a
great conference, really\! Met so many people from the Summer School
again, heard a surprisingly number of interesting talks (there are some
conferences, where one boring talk follows the other, that's
definitively different here) and got some great feedback on some work
we're doing here in [Karlsruhe](AIFB "wikilink").

[Boris Motik](http://www.fzi.de/ipe/eng/mitarbeiter.php?id=75) won the
Best Paper Award of the ISWC, for his work on the properties of
meta-modeling. Great paper and great work\! Congratulations to him, and
also to [Peter Mika](http://www.cs.vu.nl/~pmika/), though I have still
to read his paper to form my own opinion.

I will follow up on some of the topics from the ISWC and the OWLED
workshop, but here's my quick, first wrap-up: great conference\! Only
the weather was pitily as bad as expected. Who decided on Ireland in
November?
