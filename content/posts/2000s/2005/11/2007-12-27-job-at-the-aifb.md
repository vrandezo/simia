Are you interested in the Semantic Web? (Well, probably yes or else you
wouldn't read this). Do you want to work at the [AIFB](AIFB "wikilink"),
the so called Semantic Web Machine? (It was [Sean
Bechhofer](http://potato.cs.man.ac.uk/seanb/) who gave us this name, at
the [ISWC 2005](http://iswc2005.semanticweb.org/)) Maybe this is your
chance...

Well, if you ask me, this is the best place to work. The offices are
nice, the colleagues are great, our impact is remarkable - oh well, it's
loads of fun to work here, really.

We are looking for a person to work on [KAON2](KAON2 "wikilink")
especially, which is a main building block of many a AIFB software, as
for example my own [OWL Tools](owl_tools "wikilink"), and some European
Projects. Mind you, this is no easy job. But if you finished your
Diploma, Master or your PhD, know a lot about efficient reasoning, and
have quite some programming skills, peek at the official [job
offer](http://www.aifb.uni-karlsruhe.de/WBS/phi/jobs/KAON2_en.pdf) (also
available in
[German](http://www.aifb.uni-karlsruhe.de/WBS/phi/jobs/KAON2.pdf)).

Do you dare?
