Well, at least they went quite a way. With [Google
Base](http://base.google.com/) one can create new types of entities,
entities themselves, and search for them. I am not too sure about the
User Interface yet, but it's surely one of the best actually running
onbig amounts of data. Nice query refinement, really.

But heck, there's one thing that scares me off. I was looking today for
all the people interested in the Semantic Web, and there are already
some in. And you can filter them by gender. I was just gently surprised
about the choices I was offered when I wanted to filter them by
gender...

Oh come on, Google. I know there are not that many girls in computer
science, but really, it's not **that** bad\!
