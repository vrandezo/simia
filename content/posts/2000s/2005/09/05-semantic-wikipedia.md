*Marrying Wikipedia and the Semantic Web in Six Easy Steps* - that was
the title of the [WikiMania 2005](WikiMania_2005 "wikilink")
presentation we gave about a month ago. On the
[Meta-Wikipedia](http://meta.wikimedia.org/) we - especially [Markus
Krötzsch](Markus_Krötzsch "wikilink") - were quite active on the
[Semantic MediaWiki](http://meta.wikimedia.org/wiki/Semantic_MediaWiki)
project, changing and expanding our plans.
[DocCheck](http://www.doccheck.de/) is working right now on a basic
implementation of the ideas - they have lots of Wiki-Experience already,
with [Flexicon](http://flexicon.doccheck.com/), a MediaWiki-based
medical lexicon. We surely hope the prototype will be up and running
soon\!

Wow, the project seems perceived pretty well.

[Tim Finin](http://www.cs.umbc.edu/~finin/), Professor in
[Maryland](http://www.cs.umbc.edu/CSEE/index.html): *"I think this is an
exciting project with a lot of potential. Wikipedia, for example, is
marvelously successful and has made us all smarter. I’d like my software
agents to have a Wikipedia of their own, one they can use to get the
knowledge they need and to which they can (eventually) contribute."* -
[Wikipedia meets the Semantic Web, Ebiquity blog at
UMBC](http://ebiquity.umbc.edu/blogger/?p=338)

[Mike Linksvayer](http://creativecommons.org/about/people#21), CTO of
[Creative Commons](http://creativecommons.org/): *"The Semantic
MediaWiki proposal looks really promising. Anyone who knows how to edit
Wikipedia articles should find the syntax simple and usable. All that
fantastic data, unlocked. (I’ve been meaning to write on post on why
explicit metadata is democratic.) Wikipedia database dump downloads will
skyrocket."* - [Annotating Wikipedia, Mike Linksvayers
Blog](http://gondwanaland.com/mlog/2005/09/03/annotating-wikipedia/)

[Danny Ayers](http://dannyayers.com/misc/about/biog.htm), one of the
developers of [Atom](http://www.atomenabled.org/) and Author of [Atom
and RSS Programming](http://www.amazon.com/exec/obidos/ASIN/0764579169):
*"The plan looks very well thought out and quite a pile of related
information has been gathered. I expect most folks that have looked at
doing an RDF-backed Wiki would come to the same conclusion I did (cf.
[stiki](http://dannyayers.com/index.php?s=stiki&submit=Search+Archives))
- it’s easy to do, but difficult to do **well**. But this effort looks
like it should be **the one**."* - [Wikipedia Bits, Danny Ayers, Raw
Blog](http://dannyayers.com/archives/2005/09/02/wikipedia-bits/)

[Lambert Heller](http://wikify.org/moin.cgi/LambertHeller) of the
University of Münster wrote a [German blog entry on the netbib weblog,
predicting world
domination](http://log.netbib.de/archives/2005/09/03/semantic-mediawiki-und-die-weltherrschaft-im-jahre-2009/).
[Rita Nieland](http://blogger.xs4all.nl/ritanila/) has a Dutch entry on
her blog, [calling us heroes - if we
succeed](http://blogger.xs4all.nl/ritanila/archive/2005/09/02/57961.aspx).
And on [Blog posible](http://www.webposible.com/blog/) Alejandro Gonzalo
Bravo García has written a great Spanish entry, saying it all: [the web
is moving, and at great speed](http://www.webposible.com/blog/?p=75)\!

So, the idea seems catching like a cold in rainy weather, we really hope
the implementation will soon be there. If you're interested in
contributing - either ideas or implementation - [join our
effort](http://meta.wikimedia.org/wiki/Semantic_MediaWiki)\! Write us\!
