A **bliki** is a combination of the two concepts (and words) blog and
wiki. In the case of Simia this means that I try to run a blog on
(semantic) wiki software. Since it is also semantic, I probably would
have to call this software a *sembliki*? (just *sbliki*, or rather
*spliki* is already reserved for a *spam bliki*, in continuation of the
term *sblog* or *splog*)

Note that due to the history of the word, the *we**b*** contributes only
one latter to a ***b**liki*, as does the ***l**og*, whereas *w**iki**s*
contribute three. I guess this means approximately nothing.
