In case you did not notice yet -- the [CKC2007 Workshop on Social and
Collaborative Construction of Structured
Knowledge](http://km.aifb.uni-karlsruhe.de/ws/ckc2007) at the
[WWW2007](http://www2007.org/) got an extended deadline due to a number
of requests. So, you have time to rework your submissions or finish
yours\! Also the demo submission deadline is upcoming. We want to have a
shootout of the tools that have been created in the last few years, and
get hands on to the differences, problems, and best ideas.

See you in Banff\!
