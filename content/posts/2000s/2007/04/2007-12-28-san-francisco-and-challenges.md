Time is running totally crazy on me in the last few weeks. Right now I
am in San Francisco -- if you like to suggest a meeting, drop me a line.

The [CKC
Challenge](http://km.aifb.uni-karlsruhe.de/ws/ckc2007/challenge.html) is
going on and well\! If you didn't have the time yet, check it out\!

[CKC 2007](http://km.aifb.uni-karlsruhe.de/ws/ckc2007) in a few days\!
