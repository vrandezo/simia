RIP Steve Wilhite, who worked on CompuServe chat for decades and was the
lead of the CompuServe team that developed the GIF format, which is
still widely used, and which made the World Wide Web a much more
colorful and dynamic place by having a format that allowed for
animations. Wilhite incorrectly insisted on GIF being pronounced Jif.
Wilhite died on March 14, 2022 at the age of 74.
