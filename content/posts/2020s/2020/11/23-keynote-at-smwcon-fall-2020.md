I have the honor of being the invited keynote for the SMWCon Fall 2020.
I am going to talk "From Semantic MediaWiki to Abstract Wikipedia",
discussing fifteen years of Semantic MediaWiki, how it all started,
where we are now - crossing Freebase, DBpedia, Wikidata - and now
leading to Wikifunctions and Abstract Wikipedia. But, more importantly,
how Semantic MediaWiki, over all these years, still holds up and what
its unique value is.
