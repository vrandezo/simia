My friend Vinay Chaudhri is organising a seminar on Knowledge Graphs
with Naren Chittar and Michael Genesereth this semester at Stanford.

I have the honour to present in it as the opening guest lecturer,
introducing what Knowledge Graphs are and what are good for.

Due to the current COVID situation, the seminar was turned virtual, and
opened to everyone to attend to.

Other speakers during the semester include Juan Sequeda, Marie-Laure
Mugnier, Héctor Pérez Urbina, Michael Uschold, Jure Leskovec, Luna Dong,
Mark Musen, and many others.

  - [CS520 at Stanford,
    Spring 2020](https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs520/)
