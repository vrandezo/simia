2020 was a challenging year, particularly due to the pandemic. Some
things were very different, some things were dangerous, and the pandemic
exposed the fault lines in many societies in a most tragic way around
the world.

Let's hope that 2021 will be better in that respect, that we will have
learned from how the events unfolded.

But I'm also amazed by how fast the vaccine was developed and made
available to tens of millions.

I think there's some chance that the summer of '21 will become one to
sing about for a generation.

Happy New Year 2021\!
