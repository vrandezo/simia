Our new (temporary home) is the City of Emeryville. Emeryville has a
population of almost 13,000 people. The apartment complex we live in has
about 400 units, and I estimate that they have about 2 people on average
in each. Assuming that about 90% of the apartments are occupied, this
single apartment complex would constitute between 5 and 10% of the
population of the whole city.
