One thing I really like to do is to compare the articles in different
language editions of Wikipedia about the same topic (I know, I'm a
nerd). My daughter was watching me doing that, and then she suggested a
topic, and she suggested we should ask an AI to help us.

The topic she suggested was the YouTuber
[Technoblade](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technoblade), whose article
is available in 35 different language editions of Wikipedia, and I
decided on using Gemini since I got a free subscription for its
paid-tier service for a year when I bought a new phone recently.

I know, I know, by now everyone is tired of dialogs with the AI. That's
why I radically shortened it. The [full dialog is
available](https://gemini.google.com/share/f9178bdba2f5) in case you
want to read it. I found this one particularly interesting because the
LLM stated that it was wrong, knew why it was wrong, and kept repeating
the wrong thing. We had a lot of fun. Note that some of the dialog was
written by my daughter.
