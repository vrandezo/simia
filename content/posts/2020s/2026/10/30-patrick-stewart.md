When the table read of the first episode of The Next Generation
happened, it was Patrick Stewart who first pronounced the name of the
android Data. He enunciated a crisp T instead of the American habit of
letting the letter disappear somewhere on the top of their mouth. Gene
Roddenberry then set up the rule that whoever reads a name first decides
on the pronunciation. Given the profound cultural impact of Star Trek
this influenced the way data was pronounced, and thus much later also
influenced Wikidata.

Captain Picard and Professor X as portrayed by Patrick Stewart embodied
leadership for me, a role model since growing up. If you read up on the
production of Star Trek: The Next Generation, you will learn that he
also was a role model and a leader for the rest of the cast, his
professionalism and conduct contributing far beyond the role he
portrayed on screen. In his private life he served less of a role model,
but unlike some other stars his private life sure didn't have anywhere
near the prominence in the public consciousness as the roles he
portrayed have.

He'll always be my Captain. "Make it so" is such a wonderful
catchphrase, showing confidence in the capabilities of the crew, and how
good communication and consensus had worked before. The scenes of
Captain Picard and his leadership team sitting around the table,
discussing options and coming to level headed decisions, had little
counterpart on Kirk's Enterprise. I don't expect to get into the habit
of drinking Earl Grey, hot, though.

And even though Magneto was right, Professor X exemplified the mercy and
compassion I want to always aspire to. The real-world friendship with
Ian McKellen provided such a fruitful interplay for the portrayal of the
relationship between Professor X and Magneto: sure, both Stewart and
McKellen were brilliant actors, and I have no doubt that they would have
been able to bring that relationship to life on screen even if they
would have despised each other off screen, but their chemistry and
friendship off screen, knowing about it, just made the rivalry and
friendship of the characters feel so much more vivid. I'm looking
forward to seeing Patrick Stewart and Ian McKellen portray Professor X
and Magneto and their relationship once again in Doomsday.

Ad Astra.
