An old friend convinced me to watch Wildwood. It is an amazing stop
motion fantasy movie with so many incredible breathtaking scenes,
inspired characters, beautifully mind-tickling ideas, and wonderful
shots. A movie that is made for the big screen. From the studio that
created Coraline.

Prue is a twelve year old living in Portland. And one day her brother is
being kidnapped by a murder of crows and taken to the impassable
wilderness. Prue follows into a world of magic.

An impressive cast, including the incomparable Angela Bassett, Tom
Waits, Awkwafina, Mahershala Ali, and many others. I very strongly
recommend watching it.

It is about grief, dealing with loss, struggle, about deciding whether
to fight or not. And it has a big battle and at least one shocking,
sudden death, both of which might be good to be aware before deciding
whether to see it with smaller children.
