Computers are good with numbers.

*"what's bigger, centillition to the power of a centillion, or a
googolplex?"*

I am glad that Google gives me a richly sourced and well reasoned
answer, linked below:

"A googolplex is vastly bigger than a centillion to the power of a
centillion."

The Math

  - Centillion: In the short scale, a centillion is 10^303 (a 1 followed
    by 303 zeros)
  - Centillion to the power of a centillion:
      - (10^303)^(10^303) which is roughly 10^(10^305.48).
  - Googol and Googolplex:
      - A googol is 10^100
      - A googolplex is 10^googol = 10^(10^100).

Why a Googolplex Wins

  - Centillion to the centillion power is roughly 10^(a number with
    about 305 digits).
  - A googolplex is 10^(a number with 101 digits), which is orders of
    magnitude larger.

To put it in perspective, a googolplex is so large that writing out all
of its zeros in decimal form would require more paper and matter than
exists in the known physical universe."
