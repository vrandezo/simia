Today is the 30th anniversary of the [launch of the first
wiki](https://wiki.c2.com/?WikiHistory) by [Ward
Cunningham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ward_Cunningham). A page that
anyone could edit. Right from the browser. It was generally seen as a
bad idea. What if people did bad things?

Originally with the goal to support the software development community
in creating a repository of software design patterns, wikis were later
used for many other goals (even an
[encyclopedia](https://en.wikipedia.org)\!), and became part of the
recipe, together with blogs, fora and early social media, that was
considered the Web 2.0.

Thank you, Ward, and congratulations on the first 30 years.

A wiki [birthday card is being collected on
Wikiindex](https://wikiindex.org/w/30th_WikiBirthday#WikiBirthdayCard).
