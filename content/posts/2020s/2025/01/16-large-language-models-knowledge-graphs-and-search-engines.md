How can Large Language Models (LLMs), Knowledge Graphs and Search
Engines be combined to best serve users? What are the strengths and
limitations of these technologies?

[Aidan Hogan](https://aidanhogan.com/) (Universidad de Chile, previously
DERI, Linked data), [Luna Dong](https://lunadong.com/) (Meta, previously
Amazon and Google), [Gerhard
Weikum](https://people.mpi-inf.mpg.de/~weikum/) (MPI, Yago), and myself
(Wikimedia, previously Google) have been invited to give keynotes on
this topic in the last year or two, on different conferences. Now we
wrote a paper together to synthesise and capture some of the ideas we
were presenting.

  - Large Language Models, Knowledge Graphs and Search Engines: A
    Crossroads for Answering Users' Questions,
    [arxiv.org/abs/2501.06699](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.06699)
