Here are some highlights of the progress in lexicographic data in
Wikidata in 2024

  - [Hausa](https://www.wikidata.org/w/index.php?title=Wikidata%3ALexicographical_coverage%2Fha%2FStatistics&diff=2296112898&oldid=2028939807):
    jumped from 1.5% coverage right to 40%
  - [Danish](https://www.wikidata.org/w/index.php?title=Wikidata%3ALexicographical_coverage%2Fda%2FStatistics&diff=2296112264&oldid=2038031859):
    Danish also made another huge jump forward, increasing the number of
    forms from 170k to 570k, form coverage from 33% to 52%, and token
    coverage from 87% to 92%
  - [Italian](https://www.wikidata.org/w/index.php?title=Wikidata%3ALexicographical_coverage%2Fit%2FStatistics&diff=2296112477&oldid=2028938353):
    Italian made another huge push, increased the number of forms from
    290k to 410k, and the coverage from 83% to 93%
  - [Spanish](https://www.wikidata.org/w/index.php?title=Wikidata%3ALexicographical_coverage%2Fes%2FStatistics&diff=2296112316&oldid=2038032305):
    Spanish also kept pushing forward, increasing the number of forms
    from 440k to 560k, and the coverage from 91.3% to 91.8%
  - [Norwegian](https://www.wikidata.org/w/index.php?title=Wikidata%3ALexicographical_coverage%2Fnn%2FStatistics&diff=2296113002&oldid=2038035697)
    (Nynorsk): increased the number of forms from 67k to 88k, and
    coverage from 80% to 82%
  - [Czech](https://www.wikidata.org/w/index.php?title=Wikidata%3ALexicographical_coverage%2Fcs%2FStatistics&diff=2296112252&oldid=2038031815):
    increased the coverage from 63% to 69%, the number of forms from
    190k to 210k
  - [Tamil](https://www.wikidata.org/w/index.php?title=Wikidata%3ALexicographical_coverage%2Fta%2FStatistics&diff=2296113062&oldid=2038036123):
    almost doubled the number of forms from 3800 to 6600, increasing
    coverage from 8% to 11%
  - [Breton](https://www.wikidata.org/w/index.php?title=Wikidata%3ALexicographical_coverage%2Fbr%2FStatistics&diff=2296112836&oldid=2038034728):
    added 1000 new forms, increasing the coverage from 67% to 71%
  - [Croatian](https://www.wikidata.org/w/index.php?title=Wikidata%3ALexicographical_coverage%2Fhr%2FStatistics&diff=2296112426&oldid=2038032898):
    increased from 4k to 5.5k forms, improving coverage from 45% to 48%

What does the coverage mean? Given a text (usually Wikipedia in that
language, but in some cases a corpus from the Leipzig Corpora
Collection), how many of the occurrences in that text are already
represented as forms in Wikidata's lexicographic data. Note that every
percent more gets much more difficult than the previous one: an increase
from 1% to 2% usually needs much much less work than from 91% to 92%.
