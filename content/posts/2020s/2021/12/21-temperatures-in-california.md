It has been a bit chillier the last few days. I noticed that after
almost a decade in California, I feel pretty comfortable with
understanding temperatures in Fahrenheit - as long as they are over 60°
F. If it is colder, I need to switch to Celsius in order to understand
how cold it exactly is. I have no idea what 40° or 45° or 50° F are, but
I still know what 5° C is\!

The fact that I still haven't acclimatised to Fahrenheit for the cooler
temperatures tells you a lot about the climate in California.
