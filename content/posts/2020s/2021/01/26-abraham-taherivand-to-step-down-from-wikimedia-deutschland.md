Today [Abraham
Taherivand](https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abraham_Taherivand) announced
that he is [stepping down as the
CEO](https://lists.wikimedia.org/pipermail/wikimedia-l/2021-January/096133.html)
of [Wikimedia
Deutschland](https://meta.wikimedia.org/wiki/Wikimedia_Deutschland) at
the end of the year.

Thank you for everything\!
