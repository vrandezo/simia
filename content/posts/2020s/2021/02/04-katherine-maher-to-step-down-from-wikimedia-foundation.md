CEO](https://meta.wikimedia.org/wiki/Wikimedia_Foundation_Board_noticeboard/4_February_2021_-_Executive_transition_planning)

Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikimedia_Foundation) in
April.
