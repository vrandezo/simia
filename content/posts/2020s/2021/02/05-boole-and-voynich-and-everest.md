Did you know?

[George Boole](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Boole) - after whom
the [Boolean data type](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boolean_data_type)
and [Boolean logic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boolean_algebra) was
named - was the father of [Ethel Lilian
Voynich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ethel_Voynich) - who wrote [The
Gadfly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Gadfly).

Her husband was [Wilfrid
Voynich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wilfrid_Voynich) - after whom the
[Voynich manuscript](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Voynich_manuscript)
was named.

Ethel's mother and George Boole's wife was [Mary Everest
Boole](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mary_Everest_Boole) - a
self-thought mathematician who wrote educational books about
mathematics. Her life is of interest to feminists as an example of how
women made careers in an academic system that did not welcome them.

Mary Everest Boole's uncle was [Sir George
Everest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Everest) - after whom
[Mount Everest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mount_Everest) is named.

And her daughter [Lucy
Everest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lucy_Everest_Boole) was the first
he first woman Fellow of the [Royal Institute of
Chemistry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Institute_of_Chemistry).

[Geoffrey Hinton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geoffrey_Hinton),
great-great-grandson of George and Mary Everest Boole, received the
[Turing Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turing_Award) for his work
on [deep learning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deep_learning).
