I like Star Trek for the cool technology, which has inspired plenty of
people to work eg on "the Star Trek computer". I love Star Trek for the
utopian society of plenty they sketch in the 23rd and 24th century.

I claim it is because of the laziness of the writing: they don't keep
that utopia up.

When I heard about Discovery going to the 32nd century, I was excited
about the wonders they would dream up. The new technology. The society.
The culture. The breakthroughs.

With regards to that, it was a massive let down. Extremely
disappointing.
