Now that the Croatian currency has died, they all come to the Gates of
Heaven.

First goes the five kuna bill, and Saint Peter says "Come in, you're
welcome\!"

Then the ten kuna bill. "Come in, you're welcome\!"

So does the twenty and fifty kuna bills. "Come in, you're welcome\!"

Then comes the hundred kuna bill, expecting to walk in. Saint Peter
looks up. "Where do you think you're going?"

"Well, to heaven\!"

"No, not you. I've never seen you in mass."
