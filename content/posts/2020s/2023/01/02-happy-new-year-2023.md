For starting 2023, I will join the [Bring Back
Blogging](https://bringback.blog) challenge. The goal is to write three
posts in January 2023.

Since I have been blogging on and off the last few years anyway, that
shouldn't be too hard.

Another thing this year should bring is to launch Wikifunctions, the
project I have been working on since 2020. It was a longer ride than
initially hoped for, but here we are, closer to launch than ever. The
[Beta](https://wikifunctions.beta.wmflabs.org/wiki/Wikifunctions:Main_Page)
is available online, and even though not everything works yet, I was
already able to impress my kid with the [function to reverse a
text](https://wikifunctions.beta.wmflabs.org/wiki/Z10268).

Looking forward to this New Year 2023, a number that to me still sounds
like it is from a science fiction novel.
