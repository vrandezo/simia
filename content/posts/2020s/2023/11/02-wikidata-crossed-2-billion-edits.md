The Wikidata community edited Wikidata 2 billion times\!

Wikidata is, to the best of my knowledge, the first and only wiki to
[cross 2 billion
edits](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Special:Statistics) (the second
most edited one being [English Wikipedia with 1.18 billion
edits](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Special:Statistics)).

[Edit
Nr 2,000,000,000](https://www.wikidata.org/w/index.php?diff=2000000000)
was adding the first person plural future of the Italian verb 'grugnire'
(to grunt) by [user
Luca.favorido](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/User:Luca.favorido).

Wikidata also celebrated 11 years since launch with the hybrid
[WikidataCon 2023](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Wikidata:WikidataCon_2023)
in Taipei last weekend.

It took from 2012 to 2019 to get the first billion, and from 2019 to now
for the second. As they say, the first billion is the hardest.

That the two billionth edit happens right on the Birthday is a nice
surprise.
