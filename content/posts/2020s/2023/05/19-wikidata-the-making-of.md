[Markus Krötzsch](https://www.korrekt.org/page/Markus_Kr%C3%B6tzsch),
[Lydia Pintscher](https://lydiapintscher.de/) and I wrote a paper on the
history of [Wikidata](Wikidata "wikilink"). We published it in the
[History of the
Web](https://www2023.thewebconf.org/calls/special-tracks/history-web/)
track at [The Web Conference 2023](https://www2023.thewebconf.org/) in
Austin, Texas (what used to be called the WWW conference). This spun out
of the [Ten years of Wikidata](/posts/ten-years-of-wikidata) post I
published here.
