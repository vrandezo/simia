Gordon Moore was not only the co-founder of Intel and the namesake for
Moore's law, the claim that every two years the number of components on
a chip would double, he was also, together with his wife Betty Moore,
one of the generous donors who made Wikidata possible. Gordon and Betty
Moore were known for their philanthropy, and it is easy to find their
names engraved at the universities, zoos, museums, and galleries in the
Bay Area. Gordon Moore died today at the age of 94.
