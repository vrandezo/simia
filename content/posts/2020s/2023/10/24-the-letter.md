to Wikipedia. I found that highly amusing, that he introduced the letter
that is the first letter in his name.

Wikipedia also claims that he was born Đorđe Popović, and all I can
think of is "nah, that can't be right".

That would be like Jebediah Springfield who was born in a cabin that he
helped build.
