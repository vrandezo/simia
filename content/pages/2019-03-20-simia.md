**Simia** is the name of this website. It is the personal homepage of
[Denny Vrandečić](Denny "wikilink"), and reflects his interests,
hobbies, research, and whatever else he wants to express here. It used
to be called [Nodix](Nodix "wikilink"), but there were some issues with
the name. It was renamed to Simia end of 2007. Thus Simia -- in its
Nodix incarnation -- was created 14 January 2001, exactly one day before
[Wikipedia](Wikipedia "wikilink").

Simia has both a [blog](Simia_blog "wikilink") and other content pages,
loosely coupled. It runs on [Semantic
MediaWiki](Semantic_MediaWiki "wikilink"). It's canonical URL is
<http://simia.net>

The name *Simia* has several connotations:

  - in biology it means ape or monkey.
    [Simia](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simia) was one of the four
    original genera of the primates, as introduced by Linnaeus himself.
    By 1929 it was decided to drop the genus, as all species who were
    considered simia have been sorted in other places in the taxonomy
  - in the role playing game [Das Schwarze
    Auge](Das_Schwarze_Auge "wikilink"),
    [Simia](http://www.wiki-aventurica.de/index.php?title=Simia) is the
    deity of creativity, and legends sing that Simia was the first of
    the elves to come out of the light

And I like the sound of it.


**Nodix** was the former name of this website (used from 2001-2007)
before it was renamed to [Simia](Simia "wikilink"). While it was called
Nodix, there were posts on this page, illustrated in this overview
*(read on underneath for more information on Nodix)*

Nodix was started 14 January 2001, back then as a website about the
topics [I](Denny "wikilink") was and mostly still am interested in. The
[technology](Nodix_technology "wikilink") used was kind a minimal, but
many of the original elements still survived, like the background colour
(see [this post on the background
colour](Vier_Jahre_Nodix_\(und_kein_bisschen_weißer\) "wikilink")) and
the very simple design.

The name *Nodix* comes from the role playing game *[Das Schwarze
Auge](Das_Schwarze_Auge "wikilink")*, where it describes the
intersection of two or more *Kraftlinien*, comparable to *ley lines*. I
thought it is a nice name, as I considered the [web](web "wikilink")
also consisting many connections, and Nodix being one of the
intersections of the web (since I bring together some rather separate
[topics](:Category:Topic "wikilink")). In *Das Schwarze Auge* the term
Nodix was introduced under the wrong assumption that it is the singular
of the term *Nodices*, which is latin and means *intersection points*.
The correct singular would have been *Nodus*.

In 2006 it was pointed out to me that there is another meaning for
English speakers, if they make a break after the *No-*. I disliked this
meaning so much I decided to rename the page to
[Simia](Simia "wikilink").
