Currently, the blog runs on Hugo with PageMod, and planned to use PageCMS and GitHub.

For almost 20 years this blog was running on Semantic MediaWiki.

[Simia](Simia "wikilink") was run on [MediaWiki](MediaWiki "wikilink")
with the [Semantic MediaWiki](Semantic_MediaWiki "wikilink") and
[Blog2RSS](Blog2RSS "wikilink") extension enabled. I plan to describe
here more details of the technology and what it allows to do, and how it
can be used on your own webspace, but for now I just point to a number
of nice pages to demonstrate some of the possibilities:

  - [A timeline view of all posts that have been published on the Nodix
    blog](Nodix "wikilink")
  - [A timeline view of all comments to the blogs
    here](:Category:Comment "wikilink")
  - [A list of all blog posts --
    alphabetically](:Category:Blog_post "wikilink")
  - [A list of a few feeds created on this page -- creating new feeds is
    easy](Feeds "wikilink")
  - [An example of a page with a number of
    comments](Bologna_la_dotta "wikilink")

**Blog2RSS** is an extension to MediaWiki, that requires Semantic
MediaWiki. It allows to use Semantic MediaWiki's query mechanism to
create RSS feeds.

Blog2RSS was never published.

In good web tradition you can view the source of any page (and I don't
mean the HTML source, which your browser allows you to view anyway, I
mean the wiki source of the page): just go to the bottom of the page and
click on the *view source* link.
