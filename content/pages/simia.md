**Simia** is the name of this website. It is the personal homepage of
[Denny Vrandečić](Denny "wikilink"), and reflects his interests,
hobbies, research, and whatever else he wants to express here. It used
to be called **Nodix**, but there were some issues with
the name. It was renamed to Simia end of 2007. Simia -- in its
Nodix incarnation -- was created 14 January 2001, exactly one day before
[Wikipedia](https://wikipedia.org/).

Simia is mostly a [blog](https://simia.net) and a few other content pages,
loosely coupled. The [technology used to run Simia](/pages/technology)
used to be more interesting, but has become simpler over time, especially
because of the changes that are happening to the Web due to AI bots.

The name *Simia* has several connotations:

  - in biology it means ape or monkey.
    [Simia](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simia) was one of the four
    original genera of the primates, as introduced by Linnaeus himself.
    By 1929 it was decided to drop the genus, as all species who were
    considered *simia* have been sorted in other places in the taxonomy
  - in the role playing game [Das Schwarze
    Auge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Dark_Eye_(role-playing_game)),
    [Simia](http://www.wiki-aventurica.de/index.php?title=Simia) is the
    deity of creativity, and legends sing that Simia was the first of
    the elves to come out of the light

And I like the sound of the word.


**Nodix** was the former name of this website (used from 2001-2007)
before it was renamed to **Simia**.

Nodix was started 14 January 2001, back then as a website about the
topics [I](/pages/denny) was and mostly still am interested in. The
[technology](/pages/technology) used was kind of minimal, but
many of the original elements still survived, like the background colour
(see [this post on the background
colour](/posts/vier-jahre-nodix-und-kein-bisschen-weisser) and
the very simple design.

The name *Nodix* comes from the role playing game
[Das Schwarze Auge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Dark_Eye_(role-playing_game)),
where it describes the
intersection of two or more *Kraftlinien*, comparable to *ley lines*. I
thought it is a nice name, as I considered the Web
also consists of many connections, and Nodix being one of the
intersections of the web (since I bring together some rather separate
topics. In *Das Schwarze Auge* the term
Nodix was introduced under the wrong assumption that it is the singular
of the term *Nodices*, which is latin and means *intersection points*.
The correct singular would have been *Nodus*.

In 2006 it was pointed out to me that there is another meaning for
English speakers, if they make a break after the *No-*. I disliked this
meaning so much I decided to rename the page to
*Simia*.

**Semantic Nodix** was the former name of a spin off of this website
(used from 2004-2007) before it was merged back to
*Nodix* and renamed to *Simia*.
Under the brand *Semantic Nodix* I published work- and research-related
posts about my research in Semantic Web, ontology engineering, and
massive collaborative knowledge construction.

*Semantic Nodix* was started 16 November 2004 when [I](/pages/denny),
a few weeks after I started working at the [AIFB](https://www.aifb.kit.edu/). It
was a spin off of my back then German only website
*Nodix*, that also gave the name to it.
